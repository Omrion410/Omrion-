# Monetization Strategy

## 1. Primary Revenue Streams
- **In-App Purchases (IAP)**: Currency packs, starter bundles, and exclusive booster items.
- **Rewarded Video Ads**: Voluntary ad views offering speed-ups, multiplier boosts, or instant resource packs.
- **Battle Pass / Seasonal Subs**: Tiered reward system providing daily perks and unique cosmetics.

## 2. Rewarded Ads Placement
- **Offline Earnings Double**: Offer 2x earnings upon returning to the game in exchange for watching an ad.
- **Instant Boost**: Temporary 20-minute production multiplier (e.g., 2x income).
- **Free Daily Chest**: Claim premium currency or assets once per day after watching an ad.

## 3. Balance Principles
- **Non-Intrusive**: No forced interstitial ads during critical gameplay moments.
- **Fair Progression**: All content remains accessible through gameplay, with purchases accelerating progress.
