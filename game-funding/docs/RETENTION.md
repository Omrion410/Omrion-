# Retention & Engagement Strategy

## 1. Daily Login Rewards
- **7-Day Streak**: Escalating daily rewards to encourage continuous log-ins.
- **Day 7 Mega Reward**: Exclusive booster or major currency pack upon completing the week.

## 2. Core Retention Loops
- **Short-Term (D1)**: Fast early progression, immediate feedback, clear tutorials.
- **Mid-Term (D7)**: Unlockable secondary systems and competitive leaderboards.
- **Long-Term (D30+)**: Seasonal events, special tasks, and end-game prestige mechanics.

## 3. Push Notifications
- Remind users when offline earnings reach maximum capacity.
- Notify active players when limited-time events or special offers begin.
