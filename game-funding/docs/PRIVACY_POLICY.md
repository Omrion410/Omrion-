# Privacy Policy & Ad Safety Standards

## 1. Data Collection & Usage
- **Local Data**: Game progress and currency are stored locally on your device in encrypted format.
- **Online Data**: Account IDs and purchase receipts are processed via official Google Play / Apple App Store services.

## 2. Ad Content & Family Safety Policies
- **Strict Ad Filtering**: All integrated ad networks are configured to block adult content, nudity, dating apps, and gambling.
- **COPPA / GDPR Compliant**: We strictly enforce safe content ratings (G/PG) suitable for general audiences.

## 3. In-App Purchases (IAP) & Payments
- All financial transactions are safely processed by **Google Play Billing** or **Apple In-App Purchase**.
- We do NOT store or collect payment credentials, credit card numbers, or billing addresses directly.

## 4. Offline / Online Functionality
- The game is fully playable offline. Offline features include local progression and non-consumable IAP perks (e.g., Permanent Boosters).
- Online connection is required only for Cloud Sync, Rewarded Ads, and processing new Store Purchases.
