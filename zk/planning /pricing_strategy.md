# ZK - Pricing Strategy

---

# Purpose

This document defines the pricing strategy of the ZK platform.

Its purpose is to establish fair, competitive, and sustainable pricing that maximizes user value while ensuring long-term profitability.

---

# Vision

ZK aims to provide accessible services for all users while offering premium features that deliver clear additional value.

Pricing should encourage growth without creating unnecessary barriers.

---

# Objectives

The pricing strategy should:

* Maximize customer value.
* Encourage Premium adoption.
* Support business growth.
* Remain competitive.
* Increase recurring revenue.
* Maintain affordability.

---

# Pricing Principles

Every pricing decision should be:

* Fair
* Transparent
* Competitive
* Flexible
* Sustainable
* Value-Based
* Scalable

---

# Free Experience

The free version should provide meaningful value.

Users should always have access to:

* Account creation.
* Core platform features.
* Community participation.
* Basic AI features.
* Basic marketplace access.

The free experience should encourage long-term engagement.

---

# Premium Subscription

Premium plans unlock advanced functionality.

Possible Premium features include:

* Unlimited AI usage.
* Advanced analytics.
* Higher profile visibility.
* Priority customer support.
* Exclusive challenges.
* Additional storage.
* Early access to new features.

Premium plans may include:

* Monthly subscription.
* Annual subscription.

---

# Professional Accounts

Professionals may subscribe to enhanced business features.

Benefits include:

* Verified profile.
* Advanced statistics.
* Portfolio expansion.
* Marketing tools.
* Better search visibility.

---

# Business Accounts

Companies may purchase business subscriptions including:

* Team management.
* Employee accounts.
* Business analytics.
* Recruitment features.
* Enterprise tools.

---

# Marketplace Pricing

Transactions completed through the platform may include:

* Service fee.
* Platform commission.
* Optional promotional fees.

Fees should remain competitive and clearly communicated.

---

# Promotional Services

Users and businesses may purchase:

* Featured listings.
* Homepage promotion.
* Search priority.
* Campaign visibility.

Promotional pricing should remain flexible according to demand.

---

# AI Services

Advanced AI capabilities may require Premium access.

Examples include:

* AI productivity tools.
* AI recommendations.
* AI assistants.
* AI business insights.

---

# Discount Policy

Discounts may be offered through:

* Annual subscriptions.
* Seasonal campaigns.
* Student offers.
* Loyalty rewards.
* Promotional events.

Discounts should increase long-term customer retention rather than reduce perceived value.

---

# Regional Pricing

As ZK expands internationally, pricing may be adapted according to:

* Local purchasing power.
* Currency.
* Market conditions.
* Regional competition.

Regional pricing should remain fair while protecting company profitability.

---

# Pricing Review

Pricing should be reviewed periodically based on:

* User feedback.
* Operating costs.
* Market conditions.
* Competitor pricing.
* Revenue performance.
* Feature improvements.

Adjustments should always be communicated transparently.

---

# Long-Term Goal

The long-term objective is to establish a pricing model that balances affordability, customer satisfaction, and sustainable profitability while supporting continuous innovation.

---

# Design Principles

The pricing strategy should always remain:

* Fair
* Transparent
* Competitive
* Sustainable
* Flexible
* User-Focused
* Future-Ready
