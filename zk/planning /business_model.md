# ZK - Business Model

---

# Purpose

This document defines the complete business model for the ZK platform.

Its purpose is to ensure that ZK becomes a financially sustainable company from its first public release while maintaining a fair experience for users.

Every feature added to the platform should contribute directly or indirectly to long-term profitability.

---

# Primary Objective

The business model should ensure that:

* Every active user creates value.
* Revenue grows faster than operational costs.
* The platform can finance its own growth.
* The company does not depend on continuous external funding.
* Expansion is possible without financial instability.

---

# Business Principles

The financial strategy of ZK must always follow these principles:

* Sustainable Growth
* Multiple Revenue Sources
* Low Operating Cost
* High User Value
* Long-Term Profitability
* Global Scalability
* Financial Independence

---

# Core Philosophy

The platform should never rely on a single source of income.

Instead, revenue should come from multiple independent streams that complement one another.

This reduces financial risk and increases long-term stability.
