# ZK - Legal Strategy

---

# Purpose

This document defines the legal strategy of the ZK platform.

Its purpose is to ensure that ZK operates in full compliance with applicable laws while protecting users, company assets, intellectual property, and long-term business sustainability.

---

# Vision

ZK aims to become a trusted global platform that operates responsibly, transparently, and legally in every market where it is available.

Legal compliance should support innovation rather than limit it.

---

# Objectives

The legal strategy should:

* Protect the company.
* Protect users.
* Ensure regulatory compliance.
* Secure intellectual property.
* Reduce legal risks.
* Support international expansion.

---

# Legal Principles

The legal framework should always remain:

* Transparent
* Ethical
* Responsible
* Compliant
* Secure
* User-Focused

---

# Company Registration

The company should operate as a legally registered business.

Registration should include:

* Official business registration.
* Tax registration.
* Required operating licenses.
* Regulatory approvals where applicable.

---

# Intellectual Property

The company should protect:

* Brand name.
* Logo.
* Source code.
* Platform design.
* Artificial Intelligence technologies.
* Documentation.
* Digital assets.

Appropriate legal protection should be obtained whenever necessary.

---

# User Agreements

The platform should provide clear legal documents including:

* Terms of Service.
* Privacy Policy.
* Cookie Policy.
* Community Guidelines.
* Acceptable Use Policy.

Users should accept these policies before accessing the platform.

---

# Privacy Protection

User privacy should always remain a priority.

The platform should:

* Minimize collected data.
* Protect personal information.
* Encrypt sensitive data.
* Respect user rights.
* Process data responsibly.

---

# Regulatory Compliance

Before entering a new country, ZK should review:

* Data protection laws.
* Consumer protection laws.
* Digital service regulations.
* Tax regulations.
* Payment regulations.
* Employment regulations.

Compliance should always precede market entry.

---

# Contracts

The company should use written agreements for:

* Employees.
* Contractors.
* Partners.
* Service providers.
* Business clients.
* Investors.

Contracts should clearly define responsibilities and obligations.

---

# Risk Prevention

Legal risks should be minimized through:

* Regular compliance reviews.
* Internal policies.
* Security measures.
* Employee training.
* Legal consultation when required.

---

# Dispute Resolution

The company should encourage resolving disputes through:

* Customer support.
* Mediation.
* Negotiation.
* Arbitration where appropriate.

Court proceedings should remain a last resort whenever possible.

---

# Continuous Review

Legal requirements change over time.

The company should regularly review:

* Policies.
* Contracts.
* Regulatory changes.
* International requirements.

Documentation should remain updated.

---

# Long-Term Goal

The long-term objective is to build a globally trusted company that maintains full legal compliance while protecting users, employees, partners, and shareholders across all operating regions.

---

# Design Principles

The legal strategy should always remain:

* Transparent
* Ethical
* Compliant
* Secure
* Responsible
* Scalable
* Future-Ready
